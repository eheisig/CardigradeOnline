CARDIGRADES

A TARDIGRADE TRADING CARD GAME

HOW TO PLAY

Draw 3 BLOCK cards + 3 hand cards. Flip a coin for first player. Each turn: gain energy & draw a card. Play Cardigrades to your field. Attack enemy Cardigrades to clear the path, then attack the player directly — they flip a BLOCK card. Win by attacking when they have no BLOCK cards left!
