RULES
Cardigrades can be played with a deck of 10 or more cards.

At the start of the game, players draw 3 cards and place them face down (called Block cards), and then draw 3 cards to start their hand. When a Cardigrade with at least 1 power attacks the opposing player, they flip a block card and use the BLOCK effect on the card. You win if you attack the other player when they have no block cards.

Cardigrades cost energy to play, and can also be played face down in your BLOCK section.
Flip a coin once BLOCK cards are played and each player has drawn to decide who goes first.
Every turn start, players gain 1 energy and draw a card. At the start of turn 2, each player gains 2 energy, up to a maximum of 4 each turn. Card effects that gain energy can increase this maximum. On turn 1, a player cannot use a Cardigrade to attack. Otherwise, Cardigrades on your board can be used to attack. You cannot attack the other players if they have active Cardigrades, so clear a path first!

FAQ: Cards don’t trigger the play effect when stolen.
When a Cardigrade attacks another Cardigrade, they damage each other equal to their power, losing health. If their health is 0 or less, discard the Cardigrade. Damage should be tracked!
